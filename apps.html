Bug Safari Kids

Search, catch, collect. Peek under leaves and behind rocks in four scenes to find twenty real insects, then chase them into your Bug Book, where each one has a spoken fact. The bugs hide in new places every visit, and the listening game never punishes a wrong guess.

Education · Paid

iBT Exam Prep Academy

Adaptive practice for all four sections of the iBT English proficiency exam: Reading, Listening, Speaking and Writing. Score 75% on a section and the app moves you up to harder material, with timed speaking tasks, a writing timer and word counter, and exam-style rubrics to check your own work.

iPhone & iPad View on the App Store →
Education · Paid

Business English Practice

TOEIC® Listening and Reading practice that adjusts to you as you go. All seven parts of the test, audio in American, British and Australian accents, and a scaled score estimate from 10 to 990 so you can see where you stand before test day.

iPhone & iPad View on the App Store →
Education · Paid

WordPower: English Vocabulary

Vocabulary for upper-intermediate and advanced learners, taught in context rather than as bare definitions. Each word comes as a short episode: a scene, an example sentence, its common collocations, a sentence of your own, and spaced reviews timed to lock it in.

iPhone & iPad View on the App Store →
Education · Free, premium optional

EigoNoJikan （えいごのじかん）

An everyday English conversation app for Japanese speakers. More than thirty lessons with native audio at normal and slow speed, quizzes, cultural notes, and a cherry tree that blossoms as your daily streak grows. The core lessons are free; a monthly plan unlocks the full set.
