Support

Welcome to the support page for Strike Zone: Baseball Duel — the fast-paced 1v1 baseball game for iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Choose to pitch or bat. As the pitcher, tap to place your pitch in the strike zone. As the batter, time your swing to make contact. First to reach the score limit wins!

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Strike Zone: Baseball Duel is fully offline. All game data is stored locally on your device.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app crashed or is not working. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.
