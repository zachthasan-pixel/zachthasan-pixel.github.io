The Draconate Saga

The Draconate Saga is a fiction series by Dr. Zahir Hasan, published by Maple & Pine Press. Two books, listed here in reading order. Begin with Book 1, A Throne of Cinders.

Reading order

  1. Book 1

    A Throne of Cinders

    Nine dragons are left in the world, and every one of them is a throne.

    Lucan has spent his life on a mountain, stitching up what dragonlords do to each other. He knows what the gift costs. He has buried its price. Then a dying rider is carried up to the sanctum whispering a name Lucan has never heard: his own.

  2. Book 2

    A Court of Knives

    The empress is dead. The will named two heirs. The knives choose neither.

    Restored to a name twenty years erased, Lucan Corvinus is now the most wanted man in the empire — accused of murdering the woman he tried to save. With Sigrun Halvard beside him he rides for the top of the world to find the one living witness who can prove who destroyed his father, while the Order's quiet men ride the same road ahead of them.
