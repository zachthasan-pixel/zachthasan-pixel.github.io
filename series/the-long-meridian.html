The Long Meridian

The Long Meridian is a fiction series by Dr. Zahir Hasan, published by Maple & Pine Press. Three books, listed here in reading order. Begin with Book 1, The Oscillation.

Reading order

  1. Book 1

    The Oscillation

    A discrepancy of three millimeters per second should be calibration noise. Helioseismologist Dr. Anika Rao proves it is a coherent pattern inside the Sun—and that other stars along a narrow galactic corridor carry related oscillations. One node is missing, erased by a supernova in 2037. The remaining line may be a low-bandwidth archive written into stellar interiors.

    About this book Buy on Amazon
  2. Book 2

    A Reply in Plasma

    Seventeen years after the Sun answered humanity's first bounded test, the plasma ring beyond Mars contracts early. Palinode, the station built to study it, lies inside a changing field that can carry fragments of an interstellar archive. The incoming records suggest thousands of worlds—and expose a failed node seeking somewhere to place its load.

  3. Book 3

    The Meridian Archive

    The Meridian Archive is transferring its failing node toward Sol just as the solar maximum arrives early. The receiver array is incomplete. Karasu-3 is damaged above the solar plane.
