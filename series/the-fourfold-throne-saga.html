The Fourfold Throne Saga

The Fourfold Throne Saga is a fiction series by Dr. Zahir Hasan, published by Maple & Pine Press. Two books, listed here in reading order. Begin with Book 1, When Stones Obey.

Reading order

  1. Book 1

    When Stones Obey

    A Shepherd's Son and the Secret Beneath the Dragon Throne

    Once in a century, the Crownfire chooses a Dragon Lord. It does not care for royal blood. Corren Halewick is only a shepherd’s son—until the stones obey him. Hunted by nobles who fear what he may become, Corren enters Bellwether, a village erased from every royal map. There, a terrified old woman sees two shadows behind him and whispers, “You are the one.”

  2. Book 2

    The Teeth of Winter

    A Dragon Chosen and the Hostages Beneath the Wolf Crown

    Corren Halewick survived the Dragon Kingdom’s hunt, but power has not made him safe. Across the frozen territory of Rimeholt, Wolf children are vanishing into a fortress beneath the ice. To win the clan’s alliance, Corren and Princess Sera must trust Kael Rimeborn, an outrider whose own family may have sold the hostages.
