Blood Oaths

Blood Oaths is a fiction series by Dr. Zahir Hasan, published by Maple & Pine Press. Two books, listed here in reading order. Begin with Book 1, Silent Vengeance.

Reading order

  1. Book 1

    Silent Vengeance

    She counts what her family would rather bury.

    Sophia Moretti is a forensic accountant, deaf since childhood, and the only person in her father’s house who reads the books instead of the room. When she finds eleven million dollars moving through Moretti companies that have never issued an invoice, her uncle tells her to look harder.

  2. Book 2

    The Devil’s Crown

    A marriage is not an assignment.

    Elena Rossi restores buildings. She knows what a wall was before it was painted over, which seal is a forgery, and which century a lie was added in. None of it saves her from the altar, because the Vitale family is holding her father and the price of his life is her signature.
