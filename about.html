About the Author

Dr. Zahir Hasan writes gentle, imaginative fantasy stories for children — the kind of books that leave a quiet glow long after the last page.

His work is inspired by the soft wonder of classic animation, the emotional honesty of great children’s literature, and a deep belief that the best stories for young readers are those that respect their capacity for beauty, courage, and quiet reflection.

Currently he is developing two illustrated series: The Whispering Realms, following a young elf girl named Aelira into a world of soft magic and growing responsibility, and The Elk Kingdom Chronicles, a forest-set family story centered on listening, belonging, and the Silent Veil.

Both series are being created with care — written and illustrated to feel cinematic yet intimate, magical yet grounded in the everyday emotions of childhood.

A Note on the Stories

These books are written for children who still notice the way light falls through leaves, who invent secret places in the garden, and who sometimes feel that the world is speaking — if only they could listen carefully enough.

They are also written for the adults who read with them: parents, grandparents, teachers, and caregivers who want stories that feel both magical and emotionally true.

New episodes and books will appear here as they are completed. Thank you for visiting.
