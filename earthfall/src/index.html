How to Play

Command

  • Drag to rotate the orbital view. Scroll / pinch to zoom. Space or ◎ turns you toward the nearest threat. Tap a region name on the left to swing the camera to it.
  • Your satellites fire lasers automatically at anything in range. Tap a ship to make it the priority target.
  • Abilities cost command energy ⚡, which regenerates over time.

Tools

  • 1 · Laser Focus — free. Tap a ship to focus all satellites on it.
  • 2 · Missiles — homing volley. Best against pods, bombers and the carrier.
  • 3 · Interceptors — a squadron flies to the point you tap and dogfights everything near it.
  • 4 · EMP Pulse — breaks shields and stuns ships in a sphere. The only quick answer to Nhal shield ships.
  • 5 · Planetary Shield — domes a region. Nothing lands or hits it while the dome holds.

Threats

  • Veyr scouts and bombers — fast, numerous, dive straight into cities.
  • Nhal shield ships — park in orbit, bombard regions and hack your satellites. Lasers barely scratch their shields.
  • Orun landing pods — armored. If one lands it drains the region's population until destroyed.
  • Orun carriers — mission 5 and the flagship in mission 10. Their shield drops for six seconds in every ten; an EMP drops it instantly.

The planet is your health bar

Six regions, each with population and a strategic role. Lose North America and missiles cost more; lose Europe and laser range shrinks; lose East Asia and energy regenerates slower. Lose all six and the harvest is complete.

Between missions, pick one of three upgrades. Progress saves in your browser after every mission.
