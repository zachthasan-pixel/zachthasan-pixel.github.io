INFINITE LOOT GOBLIN: ONE-BUTTON DUNGEON — offline edition
Version 1.0.0 — by Dr. Zahir Hasan

1. Unzip the folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open "index.html" in a modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
   It runs from your hard drive. No internet connection or install is needed.
3. Click PLAY. Sound starts after your first click (browsers require this).
4. Your gold, upgrades and best depth are saved by the browser you used.

HOW IT WORKS
  Your goblin runs and fights on his own. You only manage the loot.
  A chest drops every 3 seconds and the item flies into your BAG.
  If the bag is full when loot arrives, you EXPLODE.

CONTROLS
  Drag gear onto its GEAR slot (or onto the goblin) to equip it.
  Drag anything onto SELL for gold. Right-click also sells.
  Drag potions and bombs onto the dungeon to drink or throw them.
  Double-click (double-tap) an item to equip / use it.
  Hover an item: E = equip/use, S = sell.   Esc or P = pause.   M = mute.
  Phones and tablets: tap an item, then tap where it should go.

TIPS
  Green arrow = better than what you're wearing.
  Read the orange line on legendaries. Some cost HP every time you blink.
  Red skull = cursed. Strong, but it bites. A shrinking bag can burst.
  Bombs explode IN your bag when the fuse runs out. Throw them.
  Spend gold in the GOBLIN BANK between runs.
