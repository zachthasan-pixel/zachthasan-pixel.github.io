## Screen 4 — test, then publish

1. Click **Run game**. Click PLAY, fight through wave 2, pick an upgrade, press P to pause, M to mute and unmute.
2. Confirm you hear sound after the first click.
3. Edit game → Visibility & access → **Public** → Save.
4. Send me the public link so I can record it and use it for the Poki form.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Blank game area | "This file will be played in the browser" not ticked | Tick it on the first upload row, Save |
| Download shows no files | Platform ticks missing on the second row | Tick Windows, macOS, Linux |
| Page cannot be found by others | Still Draft | Set Visibility to Public |
| Mouse does not turn the view | Pointer not captured | Click once inside the game |

D — Description
```
You are the last Warden of the Emberglass Rift. Step into the arena, ignite your saber and hold the line.

Beasts pour out of the rift in waves. Slash them in three-hit combos, charge a heavy strike to crack armour, and raise your guard at the last instant to parry and stun. Stingers spit glowing spores at you - cut them out of the air and send them straight back. When the Rift Burst is charged, release it and clear the ring.

After every wave you choose one of three upgrades, so every run builds a different Warden: chain lightning, a second dash, a guard that burns attackers, a blade that heals. Every fifth wave a Tyrant arrives.

- Fast first-person saber combat: combos, heavy strikes, parries, deflects, dashes
- Five kinds of beast, elite variants and two boss Tyrants, with new threats arriving as you go deeper
- 17 run upgrades and 10 permanent Armory upgrades bought with shards
- A new Daily Trial every day with its own twist
- Six blade colours to unlock, ranks to climb, progress saved automatically
- Plays with mouse and keyboard, or touch on phones and tablets

Controls
Move: WASD or arrow keys. Look: mouse.
Slash: left click. Heavy strike: hold left click, then release.
Block and parry: right click. Dash: Space. Rift Burst: E.
Pause: P. Mute: M.
```
