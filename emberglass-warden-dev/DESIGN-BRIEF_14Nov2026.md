# Emberglass Warden — design brief (14 Nov 2026)

**Core loop (one sentence):** Stand in a rift arena with an energy saber, cut down waves of floating crystal beasts, pick one of three upgrades after every wave, and push for a higher wave before the Warden falls.

**Why it is fresh:** first-person melee with a real defensive game (timed parry, guard energy, deflecting projectiles back at the shooter) instead of a click-to-swing prototype. Built from Zach's own Gemini Canvas concept "Warden Blade"; all names, creatures and art are original.

**30-second hook:** one click on PLAY, three Fang Wraiths arrive within two seconds, the first slash kills land with hit-pause, sparks and shards flying to the player.

**10-minute goal:** beat the Rift Tyrant at wave 5, see the arena change colour, meet the Frost Shades (wave 6+) and Blight Mortars (wave 9+), reach wave 10 and its second Tyrant.

**Progression and reasons to return:**
- In a run: 17 upgrades (pick 1 of 3 each wave), elite beasts from wave 7.
- Between runs: shards buy 6 permanent Armory upgrades; XP ranks (Initiate → Emberglass Legend); 6 blade colours unlocked by wave, boss and daily goals.
- Daily Trial: one of six rule-twists per day, +40 shards for clearing wave 5, day streak.
- Everything saves automatically.

**Controls:**
- Mouse/keyboard: WASD or ZQSD or arrows to move, mouse to look, left-click slash (hold for heavy strike), right-click block/parry, Space or Shift dash, E burst, P pause, M mute.
- Touch: left stick to move, drag to look, SLASH / BLOCK / DASH / BURST buttons, light aim assist.

**Art direction:** violet night arena under an aurora, dark hex-stone floor, glowing crystal beasts (Higgsfield concept art → Meshy 3D models), white-hot saber with coloured glow.

**Audio direction:** all synthesised in the browser (no audio files): saber hum and swings, layered hit sounds, a music loop that intensifies with the number of beasts.

**Session target:** 5–10 minutes per run; several runs per visit.

**Enemies:** Fang Wraith (lunges), Spore Stinger (ranged, deflectable), Shard Brute (armoured front, heavy slam), Frost Shade (fast pack hunter), Blight Mortar (three-shot volley), Rift Tyrant boss every 5th wave (sweep, charge, volley, enrage).
