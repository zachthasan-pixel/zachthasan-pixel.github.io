Rexy's Lost Egg

A Dinosaur Adventure Coloring Story Book for Kids Ages 4-8

Join brave little T-Rex Rexy and his best friends Tri and Pip on an exciting quest to find the missing glowing egg!

This story-driven coloring book follows their adventure through the jungle, across a river, up a mountain, and into a mysterious cave. Each page advances the story so kids color while reading along.

Features:12 bold and easy coloring pages perfect for ages 4–8

Large fill areas and thick outlines

Perforation marks so finished pages can be cut out and displayed

Single-sided pages recommended (blank backs included)

Encourages creativity, storytelling, and screen-free fun

A complete adventure from start to finish that kids will love coloring again and again.
