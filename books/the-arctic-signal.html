The Arctic Signal

The first conspiracy was hidden in data. The second is buried beneath the ice.

Eighteen months after the Singapore crisis, an encrypted knock travels through an undersea cable beneath Greenland’s ice. The technician who hears it vanishes, and the same governments that buried the Meridian Protocol begin preparing for a war their own systems may be inventing.

Hana Malik, Daniel Mercer, and Aiko Tanabe are drawn back together as a new platform called Atlas converts disputed signals into automatic authority. From Copenhagen and Nuuk to Islamabad, Washington, and a cable ship in the North Atlantic, they race to find the physical record before a fifteen-minute countdown makes suspicion irreversible. This time, exposing the lie may be as dangerous as letting the world believe it.

Book Two of The Copenhagen Protocol. Can be read on its own, but starts best after The Singapore Cipher.
