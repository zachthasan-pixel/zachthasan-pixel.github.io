The Meridian Archive

The Meridian Archive is transferring its failing node toward Sol just as the solar maximum arrives early. The receiver array is incomplete. Karasu-3 is damaged above the solar plane. Political coalitions demand either preservation at any cost or total severance, while the incoming records reveal that the archive was not built by one civilization: it has been repaired, compressed, contradicted, and sometimes erased by custodians across deep time. Anika Rao, Leila Sen, Elise Varga, Emi Hayashi, and the next generation must complete a deliberately imperfect array, survive a coupled solar storm, and decide what can be held when no source can grant consent. The Meridian Archive concludes The Long Meridian trilogy with a hard-science-fiction story about fallible evidence, expiring institutions, and a form of contact that offers no ownership—only responsibility.
