The Spiderweb Mystery

Curiosity is a Superpower

What makes a spiderweb sparkle after the rain?

When curious five-year-old Aidan races into the garden after a big storm, he discovers something magical — every spiderweb is covered in perfect, glittering water drops. Instead of just wondering, he decides to find out why.

Following the five steps of the Scientific Method — Ask, Observe, Hypothesize, Test, and Learn — Aidan runs his own backyard experiment. Some of his ideas turn out to be wrong. But real scientists don't give up. They try again.

With vibrant illustrations and simple language, this gentle story introduces young children to scientific thinking and shows them that curiosity is the most powerful superpower of all.

Perfect for ages 5–7, classroom read-alouds, and budding young scientists everywhere.
