The Teeth of Winter

A Dragon Chosen and the Hostages Beneath the Wolf Crown

Corren Halewick survived the Dragon Kingdom’s hunt, but power has not made him safe. Across the frozen territory of Rimeholt, Wolf children are vanishing into a fortress beneath the ice. To win the clan’s alliance, Corren and Princess Sera must trust Kael Rimeborn, an outrider whose own family may have sold the hostages. Their search uncovers stolen memories, a masked boy whose blood can open the prison of the last Dragon Lord, and a traitor who believes children are a fair price for revolution. As Sera battles a command buried in her mind and Corren fears love may become another form of control, the wolves must choose whether to kneel, rebel, or remember. Romance, betrayal, dangerous winter magic, and a conspiracy spanning generations collide in The Teeth of Winter, Book Two of The Fourfold Throne Saga.
