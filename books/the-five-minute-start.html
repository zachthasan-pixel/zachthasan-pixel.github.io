The Five-Minute Start

A Practical System for Beating Procrastination

You know what you need to do. The hard part is starting on an ordinary day.

The Five-Minute Start gives you one simple model for getting across that moment. The START model takes you from stuck to started in five steps:

• Spot the snag. Is the task unclear, scary, dull, or blocked? Each needs a different fix.

• Tiny first move. Turn “work on the report” into an action you can do today.

• Arrange the space. One tool closer, one distraction farther away.

• Run five minutes. Touch the real task, then stop or keep going, with no guilt.

• Trail and tally. Leave a one-line note that tells tomorrow where to begin.

You will follow four ordinary people as they use the method on a thesis chapter, a sales tax return, a stalled fitness plan, and a conversation nobody wants to have. You will see their actual cards, their actual notes, and the days when it did not go to plan.

Inside you will find:

• 17 short, practical chapters, each ending with something to do today

• 17 worksheet cards, each shown filled in by a real-looking example before you fill in your own

• A troubleshooting guide for the most common ways people get stuck

• A seven-day trial and a month-long plan

• A simple way back after a missed day, without a punishing catch-up plan

Begin with the task already on your mind.
