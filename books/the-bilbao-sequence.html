The Bilbao Sequence

Humanity has learned how to turn back the clock. It may not survive the second chance.

At 5:17 on a rain-dark morning in Bilbao, systems analyst Zam Malik finds his oldest friend dying beneath La Salve Bridge — a man of forty-seven who has aged nearly a century overnight. The impossible death points to a hidden breakthrough: a treatment that can return an adult body to youth. Billionaire Elias Vale financed the research, but his ambition reaches far beyond immortality. He is building a ship for Nysa, a world twelve light-years away, and he intends to decide who lives long enough to reach it.

As rejuvenation escapes the laboratory, nations fracture, black markets spread, births become political weapons, and millions discover that eternal youth comes with a thirty-day leash. To free them, Zam and the physician Leire Etxebarria must turn a cure into something no corporation or government can own — while Vale engineers the catastrophe that will make leaving Earth seem like humanity’s only choice.

The Bilbao Sequence is Book One of The Age Beyond Earth, a near-future science fiction series that begins with a real 2026 clinical result and asks what a longer life would cost.
