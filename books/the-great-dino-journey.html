The Great Dino Journey

Helping Friends to the Warm Lands

A group of friendly dinosaurs (with more realistic body proportions and details, but kind smiling faces and expressive eyes) help each other travel from cold snowy lands to warm tropical shores. Every page has short motivational dialogue about teamwork, perseverance, kindness, and courage.

Special feature: Every coloring page has a dashed border so kids can color and cut out the pictures.
