The House Beneath the Ash

A city built on stolen names. A house that remembers every lie.

In the riverbound city of Veyr, law is written in ledgers, sealed with memory, and enforced by the Lantern Court — an institution that turns children into paperwork and grief into currency.

When Mara Venn hears her supposedly dead mother's voice inside a forbidden ember, she follows it into the House Beneath the Ash, a buried labyrinth of lanterns, winter camps, and erased identities.

As Mara descends with a skeptical fisher, a guilty memory-finder, and a raven tied to a lost girl's past, she uncovers the Court's oldest crime: an oath that once promised "no child will be abandoned to the cold," quietly rewritten to protect power instead of people.

Now the Court wants the first register — a living record of every stolen name — and the House wants a witness to open it. Caught between a ruthless oath-breaker and an awakening memory-house, Mara must decide whether exposing the city's foundations will save the people she loves, or tear their world apart.

Atmospheric, eerie, and deeply haunting, The House Beneath the Ash is Book One of the Witnesses Cycle — for readers who love gothic fantasy, buried-city mysteries, and found-family adventures with teeth.
