Capy and the Cozy Cafe

A Coloring Storybook

Welcome to Capy's Cozy Cafe!

Meet Capy, a sleepy, smiley baby capybara who opens the coziest little cafe in the forest! Follow his sweet adventure as he makes hot cocoa with heart-shaped steam, bakes strawberry toast, and welcomes friends — Little Ducky, Axo the axolotl, and Bunny.

Inside you'll find:

• 11 adorable story + coloring pages with bold, easy-to-color lines

• Beloved cozy friends: capybara, duck, axolotl & bunny

• Large pages perfect for crayons & markers

• Bonus doodle, menu-design & certificate pages (24 pages total)

• Perforation cut guides — parents can easily cut out and display finished art!

Perfect for little artists ages 4-8 who love kawaii animals, boba tea, and hygge coloring. Great for fine motor skills, creativity, and calm quiet time.

Grab your crayons and make Capy's cafe colorful!
