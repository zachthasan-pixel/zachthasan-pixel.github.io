MAGNETIC CHAOS: KEEP THE SPHERE — offline edition
Version 1.0.0 — by Dr. Zahir Hasan

1. Unzip the folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open "index.html" in a modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
   It runs from your hard drive. No internet connection or install is needed.
3. Click PLAY. Sound starts after your first click (browsers require this).
4. Your best score is saved by the browser you used.

HOW IT WORKS
  You steer one gravity well. Hundreds of charged particles swirl around it.
  Amber particles are pulled toward the well and orbit it.
  Blue particles are pushed away from it.
  Violet particles (phase 3+) flip between the two every few seconds.
  Any particle that crosses the rim of the sphere is lost.
  If containment drops below 35%, the sphere fails.

CONTROLS
  Move the mouse to steer the well.
  Hold the mouse button, Space or Shift to INVERT polarity: blue is pulled,
  amber is pushed. Inverting drains the meter; it refills when you let go.
  Esc or P = pause.   M = mute.
  Phones and tablets: drag to steer, hold the INVERT button to invert.

TIPS
  Park the well between a blue cloud and the rim to herd it back inward.
  Green crystals refill the INVERT meter and add a dozen particles.
  When the red storm ring closes in, gather everything near the centre.
  From phase 2, new particles stream in at the rim. Catch them early.
