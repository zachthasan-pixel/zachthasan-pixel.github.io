MAGNETIC CHAOS: KEEP THE SPHERE — offline edition
Version 1.0.0 — by Dr. Zahir Hasan

1. Unzip the folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open "index.html" in a modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
   It runs from your hard drive. No internet connection or install is needed.
3. Click PLAY. Sound starts after your first click (browsers require this).
   Headphones recommended.
4. Your best time is saved by the browser you used.

HOW IT WORKS
  You control a gravity well. It pulls the glowing Spark after it.
  Hundreds of charged fragments swirl around the arena.
  SAME charge as the Spark: pushed away, harmless.
  OPPOSITE charge: drawn toward the Spark. One touch ends the run.
  The arena wall ends the run too.
  Brush past opposite debris without touching it to GRAZE.
  Grazes charge the PULSE, a shockwave that clears space around the Spark.

ANOMALIES
  Time Dilation  - everything inside the blue ring slows down
  Polarity Flip  - the Spark's charge inverts (it flickers first)
  Gravity Warp   - the swirl reverses direction
  Charge Storm   - a wave of opposite debris pours in from the red marker
  Singularity    - a black hole. It pulls everything. Don't fall in.

CONTROLS
  Mouse: move to steer, click to PULSE.
  Keyboard: WASD / arrow keys to steer, Space to PULSE.
  Touch: drag anywhere to steer, tap to PULSE.
  P or Esc = pause.   M = mute.
