# Brinkball: design brief, 2026-10-29

**Core loop in one sentence:** steer a heavy bumper creature around a crumbling floating arena and dash into rivals to knock them off the edge; last one standing wins.

**Why it is fresh:** sumo/bumper .io games exist, but most are one-mechanic. This one adds (1) a Smash-style HEAT meter (the more hits you take, the further you fly, shown as a glowing orb, so every round builds tension), (2) a hold-to-charge dash that rewards timing, (3) one equipped special ability (Shockwave, Anchor, Blink, Magnet) for loadout choice, (4) arenas that change the rules (ice, pinball bumpers, a rotating sweeper, collapsing islands), and (5) real couch multiplayer on one keyboard, one tablet or two gamepads.

**30-second hook:** first launch drops straight into a short match against three easy bots on a small arena. In-game hints: "move", "dash into them", "hold to charge". The first knock-off happens within about 15 seconds, with hit-stop, shake, a KO banner and a crowd cheer.

**10-minute goal:** climb from Bronze to Silver (about 6 to 10 matches), finish the 3 daily missions, unlock the second arena and the first new hat.

**Progression and reasons to return:** rank ladder (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Champion, Legend) with rank points won or lost per match; bots get smarter as you climb, so it stays challenging. Coins buy cosmetic skins and hats (no ads needed, no premium currency). Abilities and arenas unlock by rank. Daily: 3 fresh missions, a seeded Daily Challenge with its own modifier and a play streak, and a once-a-day gift. Everything saves locally (and works in incognito without saving).

**Modes:** Royale (you + 7 CPU rivals, one life), Party (2 players on one device, plus 0, 2 or 4 CPU rivals, first to 3 round wins), Daily Challenge (seeded arena and modifier, one best result per day). CPU rivals are always labelled as CPU; nothing pretends to be online.

**Controls:**
- Keyboard: WASD / ZQSD / arrows to move, Space to dash (hold to charge), E or Shift for the ability, P or Esc to pause.
- Mouse: the creature follows the cursor, left click dashes (hold to charge), right click uses the ability.
- Touch: floating joystick on the left, DASH and ABILITY buttons on the right. Party on a tablet: each player gets half the screen.
- Gamepad: left stick, A dash, X/B ability. Party: one pad per player.
- Party keyboard: P1 WASD + Space/F dash + E/G ability; P2 arrows + Enter or . dash + Right Shift or / ability.

**Art direction:** bright toy-like hex tiles floating over a dusky sky, chunky extruded sides, glossy round creatures with big expressive eyes and hats. Saturated, high-contrast palette per arena. Everything drawn in code, crisp at any resolution.

**Audio direction:** synthesised, punchy: thuds pitched by mass, whooshes, tile cracks, a falling whistle, crowd cheers, and an upbeat 120 bpm loop that intensifies when few rivals remain. Mute button, SDK mute respected.

**Session length target:** matches of 60 to 100 seconds, results to next match in one click; target 10+ minute sessions through rank chasing and missions.

**Name:** "Ringout Rumble" was dropped because an itch.io game called *Ring-Out Rumble!* exists in the same genre. **Brinkball** had no matches on the web, itch.io, Steam or the app stores (2026-10-29; no trademark search run).

**Originality check:** the closest existing game is *Sumo* on Steam (last sphere standing on collapsing arenas). Brinkball's own twists are the HEAT meter, the hold-to-charge dash, creature characters with hats, a single loadout ability, and arenas that change the rules.

**Portal notes from the 2026-10-29 research pass:**
- Show a visible CPU tag on rivals. Never call the bot mode online or .io.
- Label Party as "2 Players · same device".
- Keep solo play as prominent as Party.
- Use one currency only, with no purchases.
- Pause with P or Esc (both portals allow Esc for pause, and nothing essential depends on it).
- Itch zip limit: 1,000 files.
