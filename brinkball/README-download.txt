HOW TO PLAY OFFLINE
Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No install and no internet needed.
Your progress (rank, coins, skins) is saved in that browser.

CONTROLS
Move: W A S D, the arrow keys, or the mouse
Dash: Space or left click. Hold to charge a bigger hit.
Ability: E, Shift or right click
Pause: P or Esc · Mute: M
Gamepad: left stick, A to dash, X or B for the ability

2 PLAYERS ON ONE KEYBOARD
Player 1: W A S D, Space to dash, E for the ability
Player 2: arrow keys, Enter to dash, Right Shift for the ability
