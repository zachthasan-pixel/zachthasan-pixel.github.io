Children's Fantasy Series

The Elk Kingdom Chronicles

A forest kingdom where the trees remember, the quiet listens, and a young boy begins to understand his place among the Listeners.

In the Elk Kingdom, magic is not loud. It lives in the Silent Veil — the soft boundary between the everyday world and the deeper, older life of the forest. When young Elian starts to hear what others cannot, his family and the kingdom itself begin a new chapter.

The Family at the Heart of the Story

These chronicles are as much about belonging and listening as they are about adventure.

About the Silent Veil

The mythology of the Elk Kingdom centers on the Silent Veil and those rare individuals called Listeners — people who can hear the quieter voices of the world. Book 1 introduces this world through Elian's eyes and the first subtle signs that something is shifting.

Book I: The Silent Veil is available now. Book II: The Hollow Heart is coming soon.
